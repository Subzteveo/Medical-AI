Medical AI Evidence Workbench

Alpha2 evidence-retrieval workbench for medical education, research and clinician evidence review. It is not validated for autonomous diagnosis, prescribing or patient-specific treatment decisions.

Privacy boundary: do not enter identifying patient information. This build attempts to detect and block patient-specific or identifying wording before public evidence connectors are called, but the detector is heuristic and cannot guarantee de-identification.
