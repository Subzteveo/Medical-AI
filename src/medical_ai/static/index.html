About the search

How Medical AI works

Every result starts with a source you can open. If information cannot be checked, we will not show a supported finding.

1. Ask

Enter a general research question. Leave out personal medical details.

2. Find

We look in published research or registered study records.

3. Check

The system checks the source and whether the quoted words match. It does not judge study quality.

4. Read

See the wording and open the original study. Important limits stay visible.

5. Continue

If no supported finding can be shown, we explain why instead of making up an answer.

What this tool cannot do

It does not diagnose, choose treatment, provide emergency help, or confirm that findings apply to a particular person.
